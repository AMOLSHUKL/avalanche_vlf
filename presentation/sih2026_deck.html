The problem — SIH260104

The burial clock runs at 15 minutes. The response arrives in 30–60.

  • Buried victims survive on an asphyxiation cliff: ~90% survival if extracted within 15 minutes, below 30% past 35 minutes.
  • Sectors like Siachen, Ladakh and North Sikkim need 30–60+ minutes just to mobilize a probe line.
  • Passive victims carry nothing — no 457 kHz transceiver, no RECCO reflector, often no phone. Existing systems cannot see them.
  • India already forecasts and detects releases: DGRE's Sikkim radar flags an avalanche within 3 seconds, and its drone-based buried-object detector was airlifted to the 2025 Mana rescue. What remains unsolved is rapid victim localization after the release.

The math that decides

Correlated sensors cannot inflate confidence

  • Modalities split into Group A (electronic), B (subsurface), C (surface).
  • Each group saturates at its own cap: ΓA=4.5, ΓB=4.2, ΓC=2.5 log-odds. Ten co-located receivers still cannot reach P1 alone.
  • Accumulators leak γ = 0.96 per second of wall time — staleness tracks real time, not scan frequency.
  • Multi-pass persistence: repeated detections ≥5 s apart earn +0.75; consistent ghosts lose 0.40.
  • Posterior log-odds clamped to ±15 before sigmoid — no numerical windup, ever.

The differentiator

Finding the victim who carries nothing

  • GPR dielectric contrast: human tissue εr ≈ 50–55 vs snow εr ≈ 3.2 and granite εr ≈ 6–9. The engine reads permittivity directly from the payload schema and rejects rock clutter by value.
  • Micro-Doppler respiration: chest-wall displacement in the 0.2–0.4 Hz band distinguishes a living subject from static debris returns.
  • Micro-seismic impulse patterns: periodic tapping/calling detection corroborates life-signs acoustically.
  • Wet-snow absorption modeled as q = e−κ·ρ·d so degraded penetration lowers evidence weight instead of manufacturing false confidence.

A non-cooperative civilian reaches P1 strictly through subsurface cross-confirmation — demonstrated live in the demo scenario.

From lock to rescuer

Every P1 lock emits a complete rescue order

  • True MGRS reference: real WGS84 → UTM → MGRS conversion, verified against the published anchor 31N AA 66021 00000 and sub-millimeter global round-trips. No fabricated coordinates anywhere.
  • Burial depth Z: radar two-way-time estimate carried into the directive and the LoRa frame.
  • Safe approach azimuth: perpendicular to the terrain gradient in compass frame — verified numerically that the heading is contour-parallel (dot product = 0). Rescuers never walk the fall-line the system exists to warn about.
  • 16-byte LoRa frame: msg type, cell, probability, depth cm, radius dm, bearing, flags, mission-grid offsets, CRC-16. Out-of-range values raise — they never wrap silently.
